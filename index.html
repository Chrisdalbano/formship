Questions

Do you need access to anything?

No. We only read pages that are already public. If your help center or changelog sits behind a login, we can't audit it.

What if you find nothing?

Then the report says so, and there's no invoice. A clean help center is a good result.

Do you catch every outdated sentence?

We report what we can show: a quote from your changelog and a quote from your article that disagree. Some drift isn't visible from public pages (unannounced changes, screenshots), and we don't pretend otherwise.

Can this run every release?

Yes. After a first audit, a monthly re-run checks each new changelog entry against the help center for $99/month. Cancel by replying to any report.

Who's behind this?

Formship LLC, a small software company in Orlando, Florida. The audit grew out of a document-retro-sync engine we built for training content: change the source, find every downstream sentence it made wrong.
